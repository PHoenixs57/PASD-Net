PASD-Net is trained on paired clean/noisy wildlife recordings. The training
data for each taxon used in the paper is described below. Training feature
files are generated from these recordings with the bundled `dump_features`
tool (see README, Section 2).

Non-sensitive deployment and recording metadata (equipment, schedules,
protocols; no coordinates) are documented in `docs/dataset-metadata.md`.

1) White-headed langur (Trachypithecus leucocephalus) -- main model
   Manually recorded vocalizations (Zoom F6 recorder with a directional
   microphone) and autonomous-recording-unit (ARU) recordings collected by the
   authors in the Chongzuo White-headed Langur National Nature Reserve,
   Guangxi, China. Clean reference targets were produced with the
   preprocessing pipeline described in the manuscript (resampling, bandpass
   filtering, LMS adaptive noise reduction, and manual cleaning); the noisy
   counterparts received resampling and bandpass filtering only.
   Raw and processed langur recordings cannot be redistributed because their
   timing and content could reveal sensitive location information for this
   critically endangered species. Example paired clips are provided in the
   repository-level `data/` folder (see `data/white-headed_langur/`).

2) Anuran vocalizations -- transfer learning
   Publicly available benchmark dataset (training and test subsets were
   partitioned chronologically):
   https://zenodo.org/records/8342596
   Citation:
   Canas, J.S., Toro-Gomez, M.P., Sugai, L.S.M., et al. (2023)
   A dataset for benchmarking Neotropical anuran calls identification in
   passive acoustic monitoring. Scientific Data, 10, 771.

3) Avian vocalizations -- transfer learning
   Cornell Birdcall Identification competition dataset (Aldfly /
   Alder Flycatcher category; training and test subsets were partitioned
   at the file level in sorted identifier order, so no recording appears
   in both subsets):
   https://www.kaggle.com/c/avianong-recognition

4) Avian field recordings -- BirdNET workflow analysis (evaluation only)
   100 Alder Flycatcher field recordings (1.77 h in total) downloaded from
   Xeno-Canto: https://xeno-canto.org/
